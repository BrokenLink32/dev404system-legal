Hometown Kart - Bulli

A kart racing game on the real streets of Bulli, NSW, for Android, iPhone and iPad, and Windows PCs. Race on your own against AI racers, or online against other players. Made by Hometown Kart.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: phillipbaden@gmail.com. Please say which device you play on and what happened.

Privacy

Racing on your own needs no account. When the game hits an error it sends a short crash report (the game's version, the device and the error, with no name, email or account) so the problem can be fixed; Settings › Crash reports switches that off. Online play keeps your racer name, your account and your race results so you can race others and appear on the leaderboard. The full privacy policy explains what is kept, where, and for how long.

Delete your account

In the game: Settings › Delete account, pressed twice. Without the game: see delete your account.
