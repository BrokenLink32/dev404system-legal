Hometown Kart - Bulli: privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Developer: Hometown Kart · Contact: phillipbaden@gmail.com

This policy covers the game Hometown Kart - Bulli on Android, iPhone and iPad, and Windows PCs.

The short version

The game does not collect, send or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and this version has no online play. The only time it uses the internet is to look up Bulli's current weather (see "Live weather"), and that request contains nothing about you.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (graphics, sound, frame rate, controls), your racer name and your kart (driver, body and colours) are saved on your device so the game remembers them. They never leave your device, except that your phone's own backup (for example Google's or Apple's device backup, if you have it switched on) may include them, as it does for other apps. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Live weather

When Weather is set to Live (the default), the game shows Bulli's real weather. To do that it asks the Norwegian Meteorological Institute's free weather service (api.met.no) for the current forecast for Bulli: the request names Bulli's fixed coordinates and the game's name, never your location or anything about you, and it is made when the game starts and every half hour while it runs. Like any internet request it comes from your device's internet address, which the weather service receives under its own privacy terms (met.no). The answer (the weather in Bulli) is kept on your device for a few hours so the game starts with it. Set Weather to any other choice in Settings and the game makes no requests at all. Without an internet connection the weather is simply sunny. The time of day comes from your device's own clock; nothing is sent for it.

What the game does not do

  • No personal information is collected, and there is no sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking; nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No chat or messages between players.
  • The game engine (Unity) runs with its usage statistics switched off, and no Unity services (Analytics, Ads, crash reporting) are used.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The only thing a player types is a racer name, and it stays on the device.

Online play in a future update

Online races and a leaderboard may be added in an update. Before they are switched on, this policy will be updated to say exactly what the game server receives (such as your racer name, your kart's look and your race times) and how long it is kept.
