Delete your SnackTab account

Developer: Dev404System · App: SnackTab (com.snacktab.app)

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SnackTab and tap your avatar in the top right.
  2. Tap Account, units & sign out.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

The account and everything in it is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email phillipbaden@gmail.com from the address your account is registered with, subject "Delete my SnackTab account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password, or the Google sign-in link)
  • Your profile and every family profile on the account
  • All photos, scans, decisions, tabs, ledgers, meals, workouts and statistics
  • Your referral code and referral records
  • Our record of your Google Play subscription

What is not deleted

  • A Google Play subscription. Google manages it, not us. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, otherwise Google keeps billing you. Deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Nutrition facts read from a label you photographed and cached for other users. These contain the product's numbers only, nothing about you.
  • Copies in encrypted backups, which expire within 30 days.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you only want a fresh start, remove items from the Ledger and Meals screens instead, or remove a family profile from the household picker.
